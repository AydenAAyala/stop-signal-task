Inhibition task

Instructions

A dot will appear on the left or right. Use F for left and J for right. Keep your fingers resting on both keys.

  • PRO Press the key on the same side as the dot.
  • ANTI Press the key on the opposite side from the dot.
  • STOP On some PRO and ANTI trials, the word STOP appears shortly after the dot. When it does, try not to press anything. It won’t always be possible to stop, and that’s expected.
  • FREE VETO Press the key on the same side as the dot, or choose not to press at all. Either is fine. Decide each time.

Respond as quickly and accurately as you can. Don’t slow down to wait and see whether STOP will appear.

You’ll start with a short practice round that gives feedback after each trial. The main task has several blocks with a break between each one.
